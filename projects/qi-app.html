Qi App Gallery

Discover your restaurant in a whole new way with 3D menus, exclusive offers, loyalty points, special rewards, and up-to-date events.

Qi - Food & Focus brings your restaurant experience right to your smartphone. In a single app, you can browse the menu in a simple and intuitive way, explore dishes in 3D, access exclusive offers, earn points, and stay updated on events, special nights, and the latest news.

Key Features
- Interactive Menu: View the updated menu anytime, with ingredients, descriptions, and dish details.
- 3D View: Explore dishes in 3D, rotate them, and inspect every detail before ordering.
- Exclusive Offers: Receive special promotions and redeem discounts directly from the app.
- Earn Points: Collect points with every purchase and redeem them for rewards, perks, and exclusive products.
- Events and News: Stay informed about themed nights, promotions, and the latest restaurant updates.
- Information Always Available: Check opening hours, kitchen status, location, and useful info in real time.
