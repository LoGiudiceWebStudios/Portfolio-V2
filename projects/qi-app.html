Qi App Gallery

Discover your restaurant in a whole new way with 3D menus, exclusive offers, loyalty points, special rewards, and up-to-date events.

Qi - Food & Focus brings your restaurant experience right to your smartphone, combining menu browsing, immersive 3D previews, and loyalty engagement in one streamlined mobile app.

Key Features +
  • Interactive Menu: Browse dishes with ingredients, descriptions, and useful details.
  • 3D Dish View: Rotate and inspect dishes before ordering.
  • Exclusive Offers: Access app-only promotions and redeem discounts quickly.
  • Loyalty Points: Earn points on purchases and convert them into rewards.
  • Events and News: Stay updated on themed nights and announcements.
  • Live Information: Check opening hours, kitchen status, and location in real time.
